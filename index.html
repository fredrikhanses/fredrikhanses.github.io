About

Coding is my creative calling.

Ever since the days of the Game Boy Color and Super Nintendo, making games has been my secret dream. I trained in game programming at Futuregames in Stockholm and joined Resolution Games in 2022. Most of my days are spent where hardware meets play: hands, headsets, controllers and the platform rules that ship them.

Away from the keyboard you will find me snowboarding, around a board game with friends or travelling, and of course playing video games.
