High Score System

Friendly competition

When I started working on the high score system it already saved and displayed the best high score. My task was to save it between playthroughs. I then developed the reading and writing systems that save the high score to a text file. I later adjusted it to save the seven best high scores and show them on the credits screen.

Player Characters

Destroy in style

During the project I worked extensively with the player characters. It was a natural choice because I started with the input system.

Enemy Character

Exploding boxes, how innovative

I created the box as our first enemy prototype, but it eventually evolved and became our fully functional but only adversary. Over the course of the project I added new effects to the box character.
